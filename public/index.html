ABOUT THE COLLEGE

KURIAKOSE ELIAS
COLLEGE, MANNANAM

Kuriakose Elias College, Mannanam, was established in 1964 and is named after Saint Kuriakose Elias Chavara. The college grew from its beginnings as a junior college into an institution offering undergraduate, postgraduate and research programmes.

Set on the historic hills of Mannanam, the college carries an educational legacy connected with Saint Kuriakose Elias Chavara and the Sanskrit School he began there in 1846. The college has been affiliated with Mahatma Gandhi University since 1983.

ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

COMPUTER SCIENCE
(SELF-FINANCING)

The Department of Computer Science (SF) at KE College is focused on building students' understanding of computing, programming and mathematical foundations relevant to information technology.

Its published department profile highlights student-centred learning through seminars, presentations, project work, assignments and ICT-supported teaching. These experiences encourage learners to strengthen their technical knowledge and prepare for further study and professional opportunities.
